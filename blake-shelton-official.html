Blake Tollison Shelton

American country music singer, songwriter, and television personality

Biography

Blake Tollison Shelton (born June 18, 1976) is an American country music singer, songwriter, and television personality. Known for his distinctive voice, chart-topping hits, and long-running role as a coach on The Voice, he has become one of the most recognizable figures in modern country music.

Early Life and Career

Birthplace

Born in Ada, Oklahoma, to parents Dick and Dorothy Shelton.

Music Roots

Began singing at age eight and writing songs at age fifteen.

Move to Nashville

Relocated to Nashville, Tennessee, just two weeks after graduating high school in 1994 at age seventeen.

Breakthrough

Signed to Giant Records and released his debut single "Austin" in 2001, which spent five weeks at number one on the Billboard Hot Country Songs chart.

Musical Achievements

Chart Success

Has achieved 30 number-one singles on the country charts.

Grand Ole Opry

Inducted as a member of the Grand Ole Opry in 2010.

Key Albums

Released multi-platinum and charting albums such as Blake Shelton, The Dreamer, Based on a True Story..., Texoma Shore, and For Recreational Use Only. Learn more via Britannica.

Television Career

The Voice

Served as a prominent coach on NBC's reality singing competition The Voice from 2011 to 2023, leading nine contestants to victory across 23 seasons.
